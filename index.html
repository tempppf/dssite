What we develop

We work on the whole aircraft system, from the airframe to the software that flies it. Projects run as contract R&D for a client or as joint development with a partner.

Airframes and propulsion

Multirotor and fixed-wing platforms designed around the job: payload, endurance, and how easily a crew can repair them away from a workshop.

  • Structural design and materials
  • Motor, battery and propeller matching
  • Design for manufacture at volume

Autonomy and navigation

Flight software that keeps an aircraft useful when conditions are poor, including when satellite positioning or the radio link is unreliable.

  • Flight control and mission software
  • Navigation without satellite positioning
  • Operator interfaces that are quick to learn

Interceptor drones

Counter-drone aircraft for protecting sites and people against hostile unmanned aircraft, developed alongside detection-system partners.

  • Interceptor airframe development
  • Integration with radar and detection
  • Trials with authorised end users only

From requirement to tested prototype

Every project moves through the same five stages. You get a written review at the end of each one before the next begins.

Requirement

We agree what the system has to do, in what conditions, and how success will be measured.

Design

Concept, simulation and component selection, reviewed with you before anything is built.

Prototype

We build flying prototypes and bench-test every subsystem.

Field trial

Partners test the prototype in real operating conditions and report back in detail.

Hand-over

You receive the design files, test data and a plan for moving into production.

Why we work with companies in Ukraine

No country has more recent, practical experience of building and operating drones at scale. We maintain working relationships with Ukrainian manufacturers, engineering firms and test teams, and that experience shapes what we design.

  • Feedback in days

    Partners report what worked and what failed while the prototype is still on the bench, which shortens each design cycle.

  • Real operating conditions

    Designs are judged against weather, radio interference and rough handling, which a lab can't reproduce.

  • Shared engineering

    We co-develop components and software with Ukrainian teams and bring their proven ideas to wider markets.

Questions we get asked

Do you manufacture drones at volume?

No. We design, prototype and test. When a design is ready we hand it over with the files and test data needed for production, and can introduce manufacturing partners.

Who owns the design at the end of a project?

Ownership is agreed in writing before work starts. In contract R&D the client normally owns the result. In joint development it is shared on the terms we set together.

What is your relationship with companies in Ukraine?

We work with Ukrainian manufacturers, engineering firms and test teams on development and trials, and we introduce vetted organisations to them for co-production and supply.

Can anyone commission interceptor or defence-related work?

No. That work is for government bodies and authorised organisations only, and it proceeds once the required licences and end-user documents are in place.

Is my inquiry confidential?

Yes. We sign a non-disclosure agreement before technical details are exchanged, and we share nothing with partners without your agreement.
